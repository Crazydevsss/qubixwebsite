Let Me Bake is moving forward with its main gameplay loop. The current focus is making the baking, ingredient, shop, and progression systems feel smooth before public testing.

Core baking gameplay, recipe handling, and item results are being developed.

Progression

Developer Note

The goal is to make Let Me Bake feel polished, cozy, and easy to understand while still giving players a strong progression loop to keep coming back.
