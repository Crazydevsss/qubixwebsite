Development Notes

Our current goal is making the game feel smooth, rewarding, and easy to understand for new players while keeping enough depth for long-term progression.


Several baking systems have been redesigned and we are currently refining progression pacing, recipe unlocks, and the shop flow.


Once the core systems are complete, testing will begin with selected community members before moving toward a public release.

Developer Message

Thank you for supporting Let Me Bake. Every update brings us closer to creating a polished simulator experience that feels unique, rewarding, and fun to play.
